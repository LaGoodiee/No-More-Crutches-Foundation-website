From the bonesetter’s hut to the hospital ward — and off the crutches for good.

We help Nigerians harmed by unsafe traditional bone setting get modern orthopaedic care, and we teach families to choose the hospital first.

Why this matters

~85%of Nigerians with fractures go first to a traditional bonesetter, studies estimate.
50–60%of limb gangrene needing amputation in one Nigerian hospital series came from bonesetter complications.
44.7%of amputations at FMC Birnin Kebbi (2015–16) were due to bonesetter gangrene — the leading cause.

Sources: Onuminya, 2008 · J Natl Med Assoc, 2005 · Sahel Med J, 2019

A story that became a mission

In October 2002 our founder, Brother Linus Ochube, began a journey through bonesetter homes in several states searching for healing. Instead, his legs grew worse — and chronic osteomyelitis came close to costing him both legs.

Four corrective orthopaedic surgeries later, the last at the National Orthopaedic Hospital, Igbobi, Lagos on 23 March 2004, he walked off his crutches. This foundation is his way of giving back.
