About the foundation

Our mission

To rescue and restore victims of unsafe traditional bone setting by funding modern orthopaedic treatment, and to end the practice’s harm through education and hospital partnership.

Our vision

A Nigeria where no one loses a limb to a preventable complication, and every family knows the hospital is the safe first stop for a broken bone.

Our values

Compassion — we meet people in their pain. Truth — we share evidence, not fear. Stewardship — every naira is accounted for. Partnership — doctors, churches and communities together.

The founder’s journey

  1. Oct 2002 — Injury, then treatment in traditional bonesetter homes across states.
  2. 2002–2004 — Complications and chronic osteomyelitis; amputation discussed at hospital.
  3. Specialist care — Four corrective orthopaedic surgeries at specialist hospitals in Lagos.
  4. 23 Mar 2004 — Final surgery at National Orthopaedic Hospital, Igbobi.
  5. Today — Walking free, running a Christian bookshop, and founding No More Crutches.
